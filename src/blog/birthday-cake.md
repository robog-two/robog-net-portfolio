I have tried and failed three times to bake a cake from scratch. This year, with the help of a little birthday magic, a stand mixer, and a kitchen scale, I finally pulled off making a birthday cake from scratch.

The cake is adapted from a [NYT recipe](https://cooking.nytimes.com/recipes/1018821-blueberry-almond-and-lemon-cake) for a loaf cake. I modified the recipe based on other people's comments, my own trial and error, and using a round springform cake pan instead of a loaf pan. The original calls for a sugar icing, but I added a special homemade cream cheese frosting, because it's my birthday so I get to use my favorite icing.
