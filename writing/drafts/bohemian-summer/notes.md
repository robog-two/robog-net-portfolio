# Interview notes — "My Bohemian Summer" (working title)

Captured from the writer's own words. Not interpretation. The breakup that triggered
this is **explicitly out of the post.**

## Material (writer's language)

- Had been living deep in the day-to-day: going out with people, research meetings,
  frequent work.
- What healed: escaping that. Getting nomadic, independent — willing to say *"sorry, I
  can't right now, I have to go run off into the woods."*
- Got really into drawing.
- Solo everything: "solo traveling but without actually traveling." Built the *muscle* to
  go to the arts/music scene alone instead of rounding up a group.
- Worry: that this is *retreating into myself*, only going out when others are involved.
  Then catches self — yesterday: too hot, went to Barnes & Noble to read/study, got stuff
  from Dollar Tree, talked nail polish with Nicole who works there. "Shitty Monday,
  therefore I'm failing" — doesn't fully buy that pessimistic read.

### Definition of "esoteric" (the spine)

- A scale: one end *rigorous / clinical / empirical*, the other end *esoteric*. The whole
  scale is "sort of logic" — everything on it is logical.
- Esoteric = **internally logical but externally incomprehensible.**
- A richly connected web of beliefs (political, spiritual, interpersonal), thought about
  hard. The un-thought-through parts are *weaknesses* worked on via conversation/research.
- Not compatible with empiricism — but not because claims would *fail* a study; because
  you *couldn't* test them. Not asking disprovable questions. Taking things already known
  to be true and **feeling them out into something consistent with itself and with how
  you feel.**
- **Symbolic** is the key word: an esoteric life finds a representational object to hold
  an idea in. Learns things that imprecise everyday language — or over-precise,
  study-specific knowledge that "doesn't fit your box" — can't give.

### Astrology

- Answers questions about self, others, love, motivation, work, passion, drive, malaise.
  Psychological but not diagnostic — emotional, intuitive, **more useful than a diagnosis.**
- Examples: Virgo moon → obsessed with learning; Venus in the 7th house → obsessed with
  marriage. "Makes sense that I am the way I am in relation to the planets and their
  symbolic meaning."
- Has helped: relationships (parents, friends), politics, emotionally processing the news.
- *"Evidence isn't important for living a good life."* You could follow every study and
  still wall yourself indoors, only going out in a sun hat. "You gotta live a little."
  Living a little isn't "healthy," but it's important to a rich and full life.

### Cultural claim

- The internet + everyone being able to be educated about everything has **almost cost us
  the ability to be esoteric** — to be lost in our own world, our own art, our own
  self-created culture.

### AI + copyright branch (may not belong in this post)

- The esoteric stance lets the writer separate beneficial from harmful in AI, stay willing
  to explore/experiment — while people around them (often classically trained artists,
  who you'd expect to be curious) go dogmatic, polarized, unwilling.
- Copyright take: the language of copyright is wrong. Copyright was never about artists —
  it's for big music companies, licensing firms, publishers. It's a *license*, a fungible
  economic good, not a human right protecting individuals. Taking inspiration infringes
  no one's human rights, "as much as the FBI piracy warning would like you to believe."
