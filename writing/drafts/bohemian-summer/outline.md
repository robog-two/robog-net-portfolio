# Outline — "My Bohemian Summer"

**Form:** a bohemian almanac — a loose-strung series of small summer pleasures, each a
scene or an artifact, each leaving a little lesson. Personal essay per `../../STYLE.md`,
but the *structure itself* should feel like the summer: unhurried, indulgent, joyful.

**Mood & texture (this piece's north star):** the prose should drip with leisure, joy, and
hedonism. Sensory and specific. Sedaris-adjacent wit — self-aware, warm, funny through
detail. When we draft, run `/cw-sedaris` on it. Slow down and enjoy the sentences; this is
the one essay that's *allowed* to loll.

**Quiet spine (revealed, not announced):** all these pleasures share one thing — they're
internally logical and externally incomprehensible. A good life doesn't require evidence.
That lands at the end as the thing the reader realizes they've been reading about, not as a
thesis stated up front.

**Cut:** the breakup that triggered all this stays out entirely. AI/copyright is gone for
good.

---

## Open: by the pool

**Feel:** set the whole languid register in the first paragraph. Mood before meaning.

**Beats:**
- Sun, a chair, a David Sedaris book.
- Pretzels and hummus, vegetarian and unbothered.
- The sun-hat line lives near here somewhere: you could follow every study in the world and
  still only go outside in a sun hat — or you could just live a little.

**Little lesson:** pleasure doesn't need a defense. That's the whole essay in one chair.

---

## Interlude: a charcuterie board shopping list

**Feel:** literally embed the list mid-essay (you love an in-markdown artifact — this is the
joyful cousin of the fan-controller diagram). A small manifesto of abundance dressed up as
a grocery run.

**Beats:**
- The actual list — cheeses, a jam, something briny, good bread, the grapes you don't
  strictly need.
- A running aside on why each thing earns its place. Assembling small pleasures is a craft.

**Little lesson:** a rich life is mostly a matter of what you're willing to put on the board.

---

## Going alone

**Feel:** the solo muscle, rendered as freedom rather than loneliness.

**Beats:**
- "Sorry, I can't right now — I have to go run off into the woods."
- Solo trips to the gallery, a show, Barnes & Noble to read and study.
- Nicole at the store, and the small human joy of talking nail polish with a near-stranger.
- The worry — *am I just retreating into myself?* — shows up and gets gently overruled. Keep
  it live and light; don't resolve it with a lecture.

**Little lesson:** you can be your own plus-one, and it's better than it sounds.

---

## Drawing

**Feel:** making things by hand for no reason but the pleasure of it.

**Beats:**
- Getting really into drawing this summer.
- Possible light callback to the published "Learning to Draw" post.
- Skill as leisure, not productivity — nothing to ship, no one to impress.

**Little lesson:** the point of a craft can just be the doing of it.

---

## Reading the planets

**Feel:** astrology as the summer's private symbolic language. Playful, not defensive.

**Beats:**
- Virgo moon → obsessed with learning; Venus in the 7th → obsessed with marriage.
- Not diagnostic — emotional, intuitive, and, frankly, delicious to think about.
- "Evidence isn't important for living a good life." Lands here, lightly.
- The symbolic idea gets its one clear line: a good symbol holds a truth better than a study
  ever could.

**Little lesson:** it doesn't have to be provable to be useful. It just has to be *yours*.

---

## Close: only from the inside

**Feel:** the small lessons quietly gather into the one big one.

**Beats:**
- What the pool, the board, the solo shows, the drawing, and the birth chart all had in
  common: they're internally logical, externally incomprehensible. Esoteric, if you want the
  word.
- Optional light widening — *maybe we've all lost a little of this* — kept to a sentence or
  two if it earns its place, cut if it drags on the mood.
- Not healthy. Rich and full, which is different and better.

**Kicker:** short, a little defiant, in-voice — a Sedaris shrug more than a mic drop. (Draft
stage.)
