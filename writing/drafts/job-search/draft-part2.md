1. The best jobs are the kind that come looking for you.

In my college career, the best job offers I've gotten have come from my "weak ties." 
It's certainly true that I got less opportunities this way, but the opportunities I did 
get were a much better fit.

The first one was my summer research group. I got this offer after talking to one of my 
professors about wanting to work in the nonprofit software space. He ended up talking 
about another professor at the college (who I never had but had read a research poster 
about) and recommended we talk. I ended up joining his student cohort during the year 
and working little by little on the software in my free time. I soon learned that there 
were slots open for a paid summer research internship program. I applied, and was 
accepted with his recommendation.

The moral of this story is that you should tell people the kind of jobs that you want. 
Especially people in your field/department. This is where the vast majority of 
opportunities come from.
