## 20-second pitch vs. intro
- **Pitch of the body as written:** Stop mass-applying. Tell people what you want, give
  strangers your card, and build a simple website of your own so good-fit work finds you.
- **Intro's stated promise (para 5):** Career satisfaction matters. The best method is
  "planting the right seeds and continuing to grow and cultivate your skills, as well as
  market and generously share the fruits of your labor."
- **Gap:** The intro promises something about *career satisfaction* and *cultivating
  skills*. The body is about *being findable*. No section is about growing skills, and
  satisfaction only comes back once, as "a much better fit" in §1.

## Evidence check (floating or contradicted claims)
- "The old system is in the process of falling apart": unsupported.
- "Record high churn": contradicted by JOLTS (quits are 1.9% now, vs. 3.0% in 2021).
- "They find out that they hate their jobs": unsupported. The closest data point is
  under-25 satisfaction (57.4%).
- "Vast majority of opportunities come from [telling people]": contradicted by
  hire-source data. Most hires come from direct applications, but referrals are about 10x
  more efficient per attempt.
- "Indeed buying Glassdoor": imprecise (Recruit Holdings, 2018; merged into Indeed
  2026).
- Personal experience (the card, the email, the $10 a year, the Doug story): strong, so
  let it stand.
